📄 About.txt — Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Emily.

I'm a hair stylist with a passion for all things color! While I especially love exploring creative and vibrant hues, I'm equally dedicated to crafting beautiful, natural-looking color for every client. Whether you're dreaming of a bold transformation or a subtle enhancement, I'm here to bring your vision to life with precision and artistry. From classic blondes and rich brunettes to playful pastels and vivid rainbows, let's create a color that's perfect for you!

Please note my services are being offered to women only.
